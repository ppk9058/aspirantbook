# TrillionSkill End of Life Plan

**Status:** Planning framework; no retirement decision has been made  
**Audience:** Product, engineering, legal/privacy, finance, support, operations

## Purpose and decision gates
Retire a product, feature, or service only through a documented decision that protects learners, paid entitlements, active exams, records, and legal obligations. Define scope (whole service or component), reason, decision owner, dates, notice periods, alternatives, and regulatory/contract review.

## EOL lifecycle
1. **Assess:** dependencies, active learners, subscriptions, attempts, content rights, data classes, costs, vendor contracts, and legal retention duties.
2. **Approve:** named sponsor, product, legal/privacy, finance, operations and support owners approve a timeline and funding.
3. **Announce:** publish clear user notices, support contacts, deadlines, refund/transfer options and accessibility/language versions.
4. **Wind down:** stop new sales/enrollment at an approved date; keep essential access to complete or export eligible records; settle provider events and refunds.
5. **Archive/export:** preserve required records in a documented format with access, retention, encryption and audit controls.
6. **Decommission:** disable ingress and credentials, remove integrations, shut down infrastructure only after data and dependencies are verified.
7. **Verify:** reconcile deletion/retention evidence, costs, remaining vendor resources, DNS, secrets, backups and support closure.

## Learner and data protections
Do not abruptly invalidate active timed attempts or paid entitlements without an approved remediation. Preserve transaction records and required audit evidence; apply approved retention/deletion schedules to learner personal data. Never repurpose archived personal data. Provide export or transfer where promised or legally required.

## Communications and approvals
Prepare affected-cohort inventory, multi-language notice, support macros, refund/credit policy, regulator/vendor notice if applicable, and escalation coverage. Legal counsel must confirm notice, minors, privacy, consumer, tax and contract obligations for the actual operator and territory.

## Completion evidence
Signed decision, notices sent, enrollment/sales disabled, outstanding payments/refunds reconciled, data archive validated, deletion/retention sign-off, credentials revoked, services and DNS removed, final cost review, incident contact sunset, and accountable owner acceptance.
