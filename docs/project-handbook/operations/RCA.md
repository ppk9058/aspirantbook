## What happened
Describe the failure mode and triggering conditions. Explain the technical root cause, contributing conditions, detection gap, and why safeguards did not prevent or limit impact. Avoid blame language and unsupported causal claims.

## Impact and recovery
Quantify affected users/attempts/payments/content where known, data integrity findings, privacy/security assessment, duration, recovery method, and remaining uncertainty. Record reconciliation/backfill performed and approvals.
